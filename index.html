Architectural Advantage

Why 3D Spatial State Beats Flat Photos

Damage disputes today rest on photo albums and he-said-she-said. VeriSpace indexes every room as a coordinate space and chains each change cryptographically.

⛓

Append-Only Merkle Ledger

Every check-in, repair, and restock is chained with SHA-256 nonce hashes. Database rules block updates and deletes, so history can't be backdated.

✓

Snap-to-Verify Crew QA

Cleaning crews complete geofenced, room-by-room checklists backed by unedited photo captures hashed on the device.

◎

Silent Guest Fingerprinting

Guests get an interactive house guide while background keyframe hashing secures an unaltered arrival and checkout record.
